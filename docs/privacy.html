Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Introduction

ClearSpend ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Transaction Data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and notes you enter to track spending and split bills.
  • Group Data: Group names and member names you create for splitting expenses.
  • Budget Data: Budget limits and categories you set for tracking spending.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us for support, we collect your name, email address, and message content.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We do not collect device identifiers or analytics.
  • Usage Data: We do not track how you use the app.
  • Location Data: We do not collect or access your location.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the ClearSpend app functionality
  • To process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To sync your data across your devices via iCloud

Data Storage

All your financial data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account using CloudKit. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: Used for in-app purchases and subscription management. Apple processes all payments. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple iCloud/CloudKit: Used for optional data sync. Your data is encrypted and stored in your iCloud account. See iCloud Privacy.
  • Venmo: We use Venmo deep links to open the Venmo app for payments. We do not send any data to Venmo. See Venmo's Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers or analytics companies.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete it. You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

ClearSpend is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

  • You can export your data at any time using the CSV export feature in Settings
  • You can delete your data by uninstalling the app
  • You can manage your subscription through your Apple ID settings
  • You can opt out of iCloud sync in the app Settings

Security

We use Apple's built-in security features to protect your data. All data stored in iCloud is encrypted. Local data is protected by iOS device encryption.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
