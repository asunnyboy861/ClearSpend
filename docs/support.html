Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Download ClearSpend from the App Store, create your first group, and start adding expenses. You can split bills equally or use custom split options.

How do I split a bill?

Tap the Split tab, enter the total amount, select group members, and choose your split method: equal, percentage, shares, or exact amounts.

How do I scan a receipt?

Tap the camera icon in the Split view to scan a receipt. ClearSpend will use OCR to extract line items that you can assign to different people.

How does the debt simplification work?

ClearSpend uses an algorithm that minimizes the total number of transactions needed to settle all debts within a group. Instead of everyone paying everyone, we calculate the simplest set of transfers.

How do I settle up with Venmo?

When viewing a settlement, tap the Venmo button to open a pre-filled Venmo payment request. Both parties need Venmo installed.

Subscription FAQ

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone/iPad
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Find ClearSpend and tap it
  5. Choose "Cancel Subscription" or change your plan

Or visit: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open ClearSpend, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases". Your premium access will be restored.

What is the difference between Monthly and Yearly?

Both plans offer the same premium features. The Yearly plan at $19.99/year offers a 58% discount compared to paying $3.99/month.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You can continue using premium features until the end of your current billing period. After that, you will revert to the free version with limited features.

I purchased Lifetime but it is not showing

Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases" to restore your Lifetime access. If the issue persists, contact us below.
