## Promotional Text
Split bills and see your real spending. Smart splitting, receipt scanning, and Venmo settlement in one app.

## Description
Stop guessing who owes what. ClearSpend shows you exactly what you spent after reimbursements, not just the inflated total.

Whether you are splitting rent with roommates, sharing trip costs with friends, or dividing a dinner bill, ClearSpend handles the math so you can focus on the moment.

KEY FEATURES

Real Spending Dashboard
See what you actually spent this month after deducting amounts you fronted for others and reimbursements you received. No other app shows you this.

Smart Bill Splitting
Split expenses equally, by percentage, by shares, or by exact amounts. Our debt simplification algorithm minimizes the number of settlements needed within a group.

Receipt OCR Scanning
Scan receipts with your camera and automatically extract line items. Assign individual items to different people for precise splitting.

Venmo Integration
Settle up instantly with Venmo deep links. One tap opens a pre-filled payment request in Venmo. No more copying amounts manually.

Group Management
Create unlimited groups for roommates, trips, couples, or any shared expense situation. Track balances across all your groups in one place.

Budget Tracking
Set monthly budgets by category and get alerts when you are close to your limit. Stay on top of your spending habits.

Advanced Statistics
View spending trends with interactive charts. Break down expenses by category, month, or group. Export your data as CSV anytime.

iCloud Sync
Your data syncs seamlessly across all your Apple devices. Enable iCloud sync in Settings to access your expenses anywhere.

Payment Reminders
Get smart reminders when someone owes you money. Never chase payments again.
